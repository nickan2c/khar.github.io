recipe:
  servings: 2
  prep: 5 minutes
  cook: 30-45 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Ingredients**
    * 1 egg
    * 200g 0% fat yoghurt
    * 200g cream cheese (I use reduced fat)
    * 1 scoop of your choice of whey protein powder
  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Mix everything in a bowl until smooth and creamy
    2. Pour into baking sheet in an oven safe tray
    3. Leave in oven for 30-45 min (depends on the height you put it as)
    - You can poke a toothpick through and check if its cooked by seeing if there is any residue on the toothpick
    5. Leave to cool for 30 min
    6. Put in fridge for 4 hours or overnight and enjoy!

    **Serving Suggestions**

    Play around and see what you like!
    * Enjoy with fruits, eg banana or berries on top
    * Could even add some crumbled up graham crackers to get the cheesecake-ey crust!
  nutrition_markdown: |-
    **Nutritional Information**

    * **Carbohydrates:** 19.1g
    * **Fat:** 29g
    * **Protein:** 67.6g

    Insane!!

Insanely high macros, easy to make, and tastes amazing!
The flavor is heavily dependent on the protein powder you use. I have had good success with Vanilla and Strawberries & Cream.
