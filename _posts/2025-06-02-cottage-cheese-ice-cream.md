recipe:
  servings: 1
  prep: 5 minutes
  cook: 4 hours
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Ingredients**
    * 250g cottage Cheese
    * 2 tbsp Honey (or sugar free alternative) 
    * 150g Frozen Berries (any amount is okay)

  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Blend your cottage cheese until smooth
    2. Add frozen Berries and Honey and blend more until smooth
    3. Should be a nice thick smoothie mixture. Put into a container and freeze overnight (minimum 4 hours) 

    **Serving Suggestions**
    Sprinkle some more berries over the top. Or you could add graham crackers for a more cheesecake vibe. So many possibilities here!
    
  nutrition_markdown: |-
    **Nutritional Information**
