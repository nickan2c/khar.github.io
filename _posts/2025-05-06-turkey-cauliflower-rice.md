    **Ingredients**
    * Turkey mince (500g) 95/5
    * Egg (1)
    * Veg to stir fry (e.g beans, peppers)
    * One (400g) Cauliflower
    * Seasoning (important)
    * 
  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Turn that cauliflower into rice! Use a food processor (don't process it too much) or a cheese grater and grate, good ol' fashioned way
    2. Put the turkey straight on the pan (It is fatty so it will spread the fat, instead of oil)
    3. Season tf out of it (Curry powder, tumeric and paprika all work great)
    4. Once the turkey has browned, you can add in some veg such as chopped peppers, onions, beans etc. 
    5. Add the cauliflower rice ( remember to season)
    6. Add an egg (or two) to make it like egg fried rice
    7. Pour some water and let it steam.

    **Serving Suggestions**

    Play around with seasoning and see what you like! Curry powder and tumeric gives it a nice indian vibe.

    * I like to make a sauce with greek yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt&pepper and a herb of choice (I like dill)
    * You can mix in real rice with the cauliflower rice if you don't like it, for free volume :D
