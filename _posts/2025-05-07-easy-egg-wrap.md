  servings: 2
  prep: 5 minutes
  cook: 20 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Ingredients**
    * 200g Egg Whites
    * Egg x 3 
    * Veg to stir fry (e.g beans, peppers)
    * Seasoning (Tumeric, S&P, whatever else)
    * Olive oil spray x15 (~15 kcal)
    * Butter (2-5g)
    * Tortilla wraps (2) (high protein preferred)

  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Mix egg with egg whites in a bowl, mix with seasoning
    2. Chop veg and mix in 
    3. Add Butter in the hot pan, and spray some olive oil in addition, so you maximise flavour, minimise calories
    4. Add egg and veg mixture, put a lid and let it cook
    5. Once done, wrap it into a burrito and make 2!

    **Serving Suggestions**

    Egg whites add volume and pure protein!

    To wrap burrito, put the eggs in the bottom third and in the middle, then fold the sides in and roll. Optionally, add some cheese to the pan and put the burrito on top to sear it closed.

    So yum and filling!
    
    Cost breakdown: Eggs 20p, egg whites £5 *0.2 so £1, pepper 60p, wrapsx2 24p - total £2.04 not including the negligible butter and spray etc. Not bad for 2 filling burritos!
