  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Ingredients**
    * Onion
    * 500g 95/5 ground beef 
    * Veg to roast ( Tomato, red onion, zucchini, eggplant, green pepper)
    * Seasoning (Tumeric, S&P, Sumac, garlic)
    * 50g rice
    * 2-400g cauliflower rice 

  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Chop / grate an onion, and combine it with the beef with salt, pepper, garlic, tumeric, sumac
    2. Slice up the veg, spray some oil + season and add it to a sheet tray
    3. Form koobideh skewers in the middle of the tray and put it in at 375 F for 40 minutes
    4. Sautee some cauliflower rice with tumeric, and make some rice. (up to you. Cauliflower rice has great macros. See my rice recipe for ideas!)
    5. Optional - Make sauce - Greek yoghurt, lemon juice, salt, pepper, garlic + herb of choice (e.g dill).
    6. Plate up! Veg, layered with Koobideh and sauce.

    Persian food is very high protein, but usually overshadowed by the rice!    
    **Serving Suggestions**
    Optionally, add Saffron to the rice! I've enjoyed cauliflower rice as a way to enhance volume, but its not for everyone, so feel free to experiment with it.
