recipe:
  servings: 2
  prep: 5 minutes
  cook: 20 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Ingredients** for 2 servings
    * 3 potatoes (~240g)
    * Chicken Breast (or thighs) (~400g)
    * 1 Large Onion
    * Mozzarella (120g)
    * Veg - Broccoli, Carrots, Cauliflower... 
  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Peel, dice the potatoes, carrots and onion.


    2. Dice the chicken into cubes.
    3. Chuck the diced onions in a frying pan with some oil
    4. Add the diced chicken with desired seasonings (I do salt & pepper + paprika + tumeric)

    5. Boil for around 4 minutes, and add the Broccoli for 2 minutes
    6. Check that the potatoes break easily, that means its done.


    7. Let it cook, around 10m. 
    (Consider pulling the chicken apart first)

    8. Add the veg once it is done, and then layer with Mozzarella
    9. Once the mozzarella is melted, it is ready

    **Serving Suggestions**
    I love cheese so I add a looootttt but up to you :) 
    This is good for 2-3 servings and will fill you up to the brim due to the veg and protein content.

Easy chicken and cheese meal, comes out like a casserole but no need for the oven :) You also get your veggies in! 

If you want, add some pasta to make it a nice pasta recipe bake but I enjoy the potatoes more (You could also do both!) Just cook the pasta before and add at the same time as the chicken.

Could also easily add this with a wrap and get a quesadilla, so much variety!
