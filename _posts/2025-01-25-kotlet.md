recipe:
  servings: 10-12 patties
  prep: 10 minutes
  cook: 15 minutes
  ingredients_markdown: |-
    **Ingredients**
    * 2 eggs
    * 500g minced meat (turkey, beef, chicken, etc.)
    * 1kg potatoes
    * Vegetable oil or rapeseed oil (for frying)
    * Salt, pepper, and turmeric to taste
  directions_markdown: |-
    **Instructions**

    1. Boil the potatoes until tender.
    2. Mash the potatoes thoroughly once cooked.
    3. Combine the mashed potatoes, eggs, minced meat, salt, pepper, and turmeric in a bowl. Mix well until fully incorporated. 
    4. Pour 1cm of oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
    5. Shape the mixture into patties of your desired shape using your hands.
    6. Fry the patties in the heated oil, turning each side until golden brown and fully cooked.
    7. Place the cooked kotlets on a plate lined with kitchen towel to absorb excess oil.

    **Serving Suggestions**

    * Enjoy as is with a sauce on the side ( I like mayo or ketchup )
    * Can be served in a sandwich for an Iranian-style kotlet sandwich.

Kotlet Irani is a classic Persian dish, loved for its crispy exterior and tender interior. It’s versatile and can be served as a main dish or a hearty snack. This recipe is simple to follow and guaranteed to delight your taste buds!
